Who we are

Welcome to Tiny Tricky Logics

Our mission is to create innovative, intuitive, and fun mobile experiences. Whether you're training your brain with vocabulary puzzles, testing your visual accuracy, or rolling dice with friends, our games are designed to entertain and challenge players of different skill levels.

We aim to make every session approachable, enjoyable, and full of little moments of discovery.

Clear and considerate

Our principles & policies

Learn about the general guidelines that shape our games and player experience.

🛡️

Privacy & data

We prioritize user privacy. Our games may use third-party services such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and Firebase Analytics for advertising, fraud prevention, and app performance. Our stated policy is not to collect personally identifiable information directly without user consent.

🤝

Fair play

Our games are intended for personal, non-commercial entertainment. Cheating, reverse engineering, and unauthorized distribution of game assets are prohibited under our general guidelines.

🔄

Updates & ownership

We update games to fix bugs, add levels, and improve performance. Unless otherwise stated, game graphics, code, titles, and sound assets belong to Tiny Tricky Logics.

Children's privacy: our source policy states that our games are designed with applicable privacy regulations, including COPPA and GDPR, in mind, and that we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Please review each game's privacy policy for details.
